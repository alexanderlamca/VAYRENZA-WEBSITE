# VAYRENZA GLOBAL — 2026 PRODUCT ARCHITECTURE

Status: LOCKED WORKING ARCHITECTURE

Purpose: Establish the 48-SKU product framework before final product photography, dimensions, materials, finishes, packaging and commercial specifications are confirmed.

Important rule: The SKU structure is permanent unless a future product-development decision requires a controlled revision. Current product names are functional working categories, not claims about final specifications.

## Architecture Rules

- Total launch architecture: 48 SKUs.
- Three collection families only: VZ-S, VZ-T, VZ-P.
- Series names appear once at collection-section level and should not be repeated on every product card.
- Future product cards should show: SKU + product type/name + status/CTA.
- Do not publish invented dimensions, materials, MOQ, load ratings, finishes or packaging data.
- Final verified products will be assigned into the nearest matching reserved SKU slot.
- Recommended future URL pattern: `/products/vz-s01/`, `/products/vz-t01/`, `/products/vz-p01/`.

## Future Product-Card Rule

Collection page structure should be:

1. Show the series heading once.
2. Under it, show SKU cards without repeating the series name.
3. Each pre-product card should contain only:
   - SKU
   - Working product category
   - `DETAILS IN DEVELOPMENT` or equivalent localized status
4. When a real product is approved, replace the placeholder status with:
   - verified product image
   - final product name
   - verified core specifications
   - `VIEW PRODUCT` and/or `REQUEST A QUOTE`

Example:

`VZ-S09`

`CLASSIC SUIT HANGER`

`DETAILS IN DEVELOPMENT`

Do not render `VZ-S SERIES` again inside that card.
